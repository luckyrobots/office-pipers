The pack metadata available for this snapshot did not provide an upstream source/license. This repository therefore does not relabel those assets as MIT, Apache, BSD or public domain. Their presence in this project is not a separate license grant for unrelated redistribution or use. Ask Lucky Robots for the applicable content-pack terms.

Lucky Engine itself is a separate dependency and is not redistributed here. Its C# API/runtime binaries and private source patches are excluded.

## Wood-filler tube and artwork

The reference object is a Minwax Stainable Wood Filler tube. Minwax names, logos and packaging graphics belong to their respective owners; they are used as visual references for this experiment, without an endorsement claim.

The user supplied the product photos and [Scaniverse reference scan](https://scaniverse.com/scan/356vimoazqajzk66). `MINWAX-SCAN-PROVENANCE.json` records that reference, dimensions and derived scan resources. The newer native POD geometry, editable body/cap parts and reconstructed label resources have their own source/identity records under `assets/minwax-native-v2/`. The public snapshot retains those records; it does not assert a general third-party scan or trademark license.
